Privacy and cookies

Italiano · English · Français · Español · Deutsch

Last updated: 21 October 2026.

This page explains which personal data the website daniel-lippi.github.io processes and how, under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and the Italian Legislative Decree 196/2003 (Privacy Code). The reference text is the Italian one. This English version is provided for convenience and applies to all language versions of the site.

1. Data controller

The data controller is Daniel Lippi, author of the site. For any request about personal data, you can write to him through the profiles listed in the Contact section (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn).

2. Cookies

This site does not use cookies: no technical cookies, no profiling cookies and no third-party cookies. For this reason there is no banner and you are not asked for any consent.

The site saves one piece of information in your browser: the language you choose with the flags at the top (Italian, English, French, German, Spanish), using the local storage of the browser (localStorage). It is only used to remember your choice on your next visit, it stays on your device and it is not sent to anyone. You can delete it at any time from your browser settings.

3. Visit statistics (GoatCounter)

To understand how many people read the site and which pages interest them, I use GoatCounter (goatcounter.com), a statistics service built to respect privacy. GoatCounter does not use cookies, does not follow you from one site to another and does not build personal profiles.

When you visit a page, the service records technical and statistical data: the page you visited, the site you came from, the type of browser and operating system, the screen size, the country you are in and the language of your browser. Your full IP address is not stored. To tell a visitor from a page reload, the service calculates a temporary anonymous code, which lasts at most 8 hours.

I use this data only in aggregate form, to improve the content of the site. I do not use it to identify you and I do not combine it with other information.

4. Hosting and other technical services

GitHub Pages. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (Microsoft group). Like any hosting service, GitHub may record technical access data, including the IP address, to run the site and keep it secure. GitHub processes this data under its own privacy statement.

Google Fonts. The fonts of the site are loaded from Google servers (Google Fonts). When you open a page, your browser contacts Google and may send your IP address. This does not use cookies. Google processes the data under its own privacy policy.

Links to other sites. The site contains links to Amazon, Kobo, BookTribu, Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn. They are simple links: the site does not embed plugins, buttons or content from these services. If you click a link, you leave this site and the processing of your data depends on the privacy policy of the destination site.

5. Data you send me voluntarily

The site has no contact forms, newsletter or comments. If you write to me through social media profiles, your messages are processed by the platform you choose, under its rules, and by me, only to reply to you.

6. Purposes and legal basis

I process the data described above for three purposes. The first is to run the site and keep it secure. The second is to obtain anonymous and aggregate statistics about visits. The third is to reply to messages you send me. For the first two, the legal basis is the legitimate interest of the controller (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), which is to run a website and to know in general terms what readers are interested in, with a very small effect on your privacy. For the third, it is the performance of your request (Article 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR).

You are not required to provide any data: you can browse the site without entering personal information.

7. Data retention

Statistics stay in the GoatCounter account as long as I keep it active, unless I delete them earlier. Technical hosting data is kept by GitHub and Google under their own policies. Messages received through social media are kept for the time needed to reply to you.

8. Recipients and transfers outside the European Union

Your data is not sold or given to third parties for commercial purposes. It is processed by the providers named above (GoatCounter, GitHub, Google) as processors or as independent controllers for their own services. Some of them, such as GitHub and Google, are based in the United States: transfers take place with the safeguards required by the GDPR, which are described in their own privacy statements.

9. Your rights

You have the right to ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing and portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Articles 15 to 22 GDPR). Because the statistics are anonymous and cannot identify you, in many cases I will not be able to link them to you (Article 11 GDPR). To use your rights, write to me through the profiles listed in point 1.

If you think the processing breaks the law, you can complain to the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, garanteprivacy.it) or to the authority of the EU country where you live.

10. Changes to this page

I may update this notice, for example if I change the services I use. The date at the top shows the last change.
